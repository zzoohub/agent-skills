# Renewal Playbook

Read for sales-led or contracted renewals, or to save one account at risk. Output: § Renewals in `churn-prevention.md` (default `biz/growth/churn-prevention.md`), ≤600 words, or one account's save plan, ≤400 words, inline unless the caller names a file. Sections are a menu: cadence · risk and evidence · plays with owners and dates · concessions · walk-away.

## Cadence

Count back from the non-renewal notice deadline, not the renewal date: T-120 risk review (multi-year or procurement-led: T-180); T-90 value review with the economic buyer against the outcomes agreed at sale; T-60 commercial terms proposed, leaving procurement time. Keep ≥2 engaged contacts per account; a champion's or sponsor's departure escalates that week.

## Saving one account

Classify the risk from evidence (usage against paid seats, outcomes agreed at sale, contacts, support history), not from the owner's label, then run its play:
- **Value** (agreed outcomes unmet, usage flat or falling): a dated remediation plan, owned on both sides, that lands before the notice deadline.
- **Relationship** (champion or sponsor gone, no executive contact): an executive-to-executive meeting within two weeks and a new champion named.
- **Budget** (budget cut, low seat utilization): rescope seats or modules before touching unit price.
- **Competitor** (an evaluation or RFP under way): outcome evidence and switching cost; roadmap only as dated, written commitments from product.

Every concession is a trade: price for term length, prepay, a reference or reduced scope; price terms come from pricing, if available. Discount approval sits above whoever is measured on saves. Name the walk-away before the first call: the renewal size below which the account costs more to serve than it brings.
