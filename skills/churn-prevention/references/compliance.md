# Subscription Compliance

As of 2026-10-08. Not legal advice; verify each row against its source before launch. Unless you route by customer location, serve the strictest rule. Notice windows are ranges counted from different anchors (renewal or the cancellation deadline), so the strictest is their overlap: a ≥1-year term sold in every state below, cancellable until renewal, fits all of them only on day 30; where no overlap exists, route by customer location.

## US federal

The FTC's amended Negative Option Rule ("click to cancel") was vacated by the 8th Circuit on 2025-07-08; an ANPRM followed on 2026-03-11, with no proposed rule yet. Enforcement continues under FTC Act §5 and ROSCA, which requires material terms disclosed before taking billing information, express informed consent before charging, and a simple way to stop recurring charges. The Amazon Prime settlement (September 2025, $2.5B) sets the practical bar: a clear way to decline, cancellation as easy as signup.

Signup: terms beside the consent control, affirmative consent before charging, an acknowledgment with the terms and how to cancel (California); Vermont adds a separate auto-renewal opt-in. Keep proof of consent 3 years, or 1 year after termination if longer (California), and records of cancellation requests (NYC). Not exhaustive: check each state you serve.

## EU and national

- **EU:** the UCPD and CRD, as national law, bind any business selling to consumers; the DSA's dark-pattern ban (Art. 25) reaches only online platforms and defers to the UCPD, the DMA's only gatekeepers. Digital Fairness Act: proposal expected Q4 2026.
- **Withdrawal button** (CRD Art. 11a, from 2026-06-19): consumer contracts concluded online need a "withdraw from contract here" function (or equally clear wording), prominent for the whole 14-day withdrawal period and distinct from any general service or cancel flow.
- **Germany, § 312k BGB** (since 2022-07-01): an always-available cancel button leads to a confirmation page holding only the statutory fields and the confirm button; a "pause instead" offer there is unlawful (BGH I ZR 200/25, 2026-07-16). Login before cancelling: unlawful per the Kammergericht (November 2025); BGH hearing 2026-11-05 (I ZR 272/25, 275/25).
- **France, Code de la consommation L215-1-1** (since 2023-06-01): contracts that can be concluded online must be terminable online through a free, direct, permanently accessible function ("three clicks").

## UK

DMCC Act 2024: CMA direct fines for unfair practices since 2025-04-06. Its subscription-contracts regime (pre-contract information, renewal reminders, cooling-off, easy exit) starts January 2027; secondary legislation and guidance were still pending in September 2026, so confirm.
