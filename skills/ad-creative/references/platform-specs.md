# Platform Specs

Scope: the limits, sizes and counting rules to check before export, and how a concept adapts across platforms and languages. As of 2026-10; limits change without notice, so verify each on its live spec page. Visible length is the hook's budget; maximum length is only what the platform accepts.

## Counting characters

Count the way the platform counts; when unsure, read the counter in the platform's own field. Google Ads counts each Korean, Japanese or Chinese character as 2 (support.google.com/google-ads/answer/12437745).

> Korean RSA headline "세금 걱정 없는 프리랜서 정산" is 16 characters, but Google Ads counts it as 28 (12 Hangul × 2 + 4 spaces): write (28/30), never (16/30).

## Adapting across platforms and languages

Only the concept travels; the opening, voice, proof type and CTA intensity are rewritten natively:
- Meta static → TikTok: the primary text becomes a creator's spoken hook over a screen recording.
- Consumer ad → LinkedIn: the pain becomes a professional cost, the proof a peer company's result.
- Across languages: carry the hook's intent, not its words; adapt humor and proof norms; recount every limit after translation (text expansion via the i18n capability, if available).

## Google Ads

Sources: support.google.com/google-ads/answer/7684791 (RSA), /14528220 (PMax).

| Asset | Characters | How many |
|---|---|---|
| RSA headline | 30 | 3–15 |
| RSA description | 90 | 2–4 |
| RSA path | 15 each | 2 |
| PMax headline | 30; at least one ≤15 | 3–15 |
| PMax long headline | 90 | 1–5 |
| PMax description | 90 | 2–5 |
| PMax business name | 25 | 1 |

- PMax per asset group: up to 20 images (1.91:1 1200×628, 1:1 1200×1200, 4:5 960×1200), 5 logos (1:1 1200×1200, 4:1 1200×300) and 15 videos; with no video, Google may generate one from the group's assets.
- A line pinned to headline 1, headline 2 or description 1 always shows; every pin cuts the combinations Google can test.
- Text customization (formerly automatically created assets) writes headlines and descriptions from your landing page and assets: on by default in PMax (support.google.com/google-ads/answer/10724897); from September 2026, on in Search campaigns moving to AI Max from automatically created assets (campaign-level broad match moves with only search term matching on); from February 2027, on in Dynamic Search Ads moving to AI Max, with final URL expansion, which can send the click to another page on your domain (blog.google/products/ads-commerce/dsa-upgrade-to-ai-max-2026/). Check every campaign before export.
- Demand Gen replaced Video action campaigns, the last upgraded by April 2026 (support.google.com/google-ads/answer/15110871); take its asset limits from the live spec page.
- YouTube: in-stream ads can be skipped after 5 s and render 16:9 on TV and desktop; bumper ads are non-skippable and 6 s or shorter; Shorts are 9:16.

## Meta (Facebook, Instagram)

Source: the Meta Ads Guide (facebook.com/business/ads-guide), per placement.

| Placement | Visible text | Recommended size | Video length |
|---|---|---|---|
| Facebook Feed | primary text 50–150; headline 27 | 4:5, 1440×1800 | — |
| Instagram Stories | primary text 125 | 9:16, 1440×2560 | 1 s–60 min |
| Instagram Reels | primary text 44 | 9:16, 1440×2560 | 0 s–15 min |

- **9:16 safe zone** (Instagram Stories and Reels): keep the top 14%, the bottom 35% and 6% on each side free of text, logos and key elements; elsewhere, use the platform's own overlay.
- Advantage+ sales campaigns replaced Advantage+ shopping campaigns in 2025.

## TikTok

Source: ads.tiktok.com/help/article/video-ads-specifications.

- Ad text: the spec page sets no limit, so read the field's counter (secondary sources: 100 characters, 50 in CJK); no links, @ mentions or hashtags in non-Spark ad text.
- Display name: 20 characters, 10 in CJK.
- Non-Spark video: 9:16 recommended (minimum 540×960), 16:9 (960×540) or 1:1 (640×640); up to 10 min and 500 MB. Spark Ads (an organic post run as the ad, yours or a creator's with their authorization) have no length limit.
- Safe zone: varies with ratio, caption length and format (the right-side button rail and the caption cover the frame); use TikTok's safe-zone template.

## LinkedIn

Sources: linkedin.com/help/lms/answer/a426534 and business.linkedin.com/advertise/ads/sponsored-content/single-image-ads-specs (single image); linkedin.com/help/lms/answer/a421723 (messaging).

- Single image: intro text 150 before truncation; headline 70; description ≤70 to fit both spec pages (the help page allows 100 before truncation, 300 max), shown only on the Audience Network or beside images under 200 px wide; images 1.91:1 (1200×628), 1:1 (1200×1200) or 4:5 (720×900, mobile only).
- Sponsored Messaging (message and conversation ads): in the EEA and Switzerland, only members who opted in receive it.
- Thought Leader Ads run a member's post from their own profile.

## Catalog and retail media

- **Catalog ads** (Meta Advantage+ catalog ads, Google Shopping and PMax feeds): the feed is the creative; write product titles for the query and the scroll, then test the lead image and overlays (price, offer, rating), not body copy.
- **Retail media** (Amazon Ads, Walmart Connect, Instacart and the like): close to search; title and keyword relevance first, then a tight headline; each retailer sets its own specs and policies.
