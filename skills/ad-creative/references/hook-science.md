# Hooks

Scope: hook metrics, the funnel that locates the weak part of an ad, the three-track opening, and the palette to draft hooks from: archetypes and each platform's native formats. Which hook to write comes from the concept (SKILL.md Stage 2); whether the creative is the problem at all is SKILL.md § Diagnose.

## Metrics

- **Hook rate** = the platform's earliest view metric ÷ impressions: 3-second video plays on Meta, 2-second video views on TikTok.
- **Hold rate** = a later view metric ÷ that earliest one: ThruPlays on Meta, 6-second video views on TikTok.
- Definitions differ by platform and get renamed or retired: confirm the metric names in the current reporting UI (as of 2026-10). Compare only within one platform and placement, against the account's own baseline (SKILL.md § Diagnose).
- Static ads have no hook rate: CTR is the first signal, read beside CVR.

## The funnel

Once SKILL.md § Diagnose has ruled out causes outside the creative, read the signals in order; the first weak one is the part to fix. They locate the problem; the judged outcome per dollar decides which ad wins.

| Signal | Fix |
|---|---|
| Low hook rate | the opening: frame, line, VO |
| Hook fine, low hold | pacing or payoff |
| Hold fine, low CTR | offer or CTA |
| CTR fine, low CVR | message match, or the hook is bait |
| All fine, CPA high | economics (price, offer, margin): not creative; the pricing capability, if available |
| One ad's CPM up while its peers hold | down-ranked (engagement bait, negative feedback, policy limits): its diagnostics, not fatigue |

## The opening: three tracks

| Track | Its job | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| First frame | stop the thumb | motion, a face, a mid-action start or the product in use; inside the safe zone; never a logo card |
| On-screen text | carry the hook with sound off | about seven words or fewer, readable in a second |
| VO, first line | carry the hook with sound on | the promise in the first sentence; no greeting, no brand intro |

Static: the image is the frame and the first line of text is the text track. On LinkedIn the hook is the intro text before the "see more" cut; on display, the whole ad, read in under 2 s. Search has no hook: write message types instead (`references/copywriting-frameworks.md`).

## Palette: archetypes

Draft from several rows, then keep the hooks that best fit the concept's person and belief. Write slots, never realistic numbers: "You're losing [SOURCE NEEDED: hours per week] to manual data entry."

| Archetype | One example shape | Bound by |
|---|---|---|
| Problem call-out | "Still [workaround] every [time]?" | roles only, never "you" + a sensitive attribute |
| Objection first | "'[Top objection, in customers' words].' Fair. Here's [answer]." | the answer is true and shown |
| Direct address | "[Role]s who [specific situation]: this one's for you." | roles only |
| Surprising number | "[Number on file] of [group] [unexpected fact]" | evidence on file; typical |
| Social proof | "[Count on file] [role]s switched from [alternative]" | evidence on file |
| Before/after | "[Before state] → [after state] in [time on file]" | typical; restricted for health, weight and cosmetics |
| Demonstration | the product doing the thing in the first 3 s, a 3-word caption | the visual is the hook |
| Curiosity gap | "The [setting or step] most [role]s skip" | pays off within ~5 s, or it is bait |
| Contrarian | "Stop [common advice]. Here's why [mechanism]." | a real mechanism behind it |
| Story | "I [situation] until [discovery]…" | a real customer; testimonial rules |
| Question | "What would you do with [benefit] back?" | answerable in their head; no sensitive attributes |
| Pattern interrupt | start mid-sentence or mid-action, bridge to the message within ~2 s | the bridge, or it is bait |
| Disqualifier | "Don't buy [product] if [disqualifier]." | qualifies on purpose: judge cost per qualified result |
| Real deadline | "[Offer] ends [date]" | only when the deadline is real |

## Palette: native formats

Copy the form of each platform's own top organic posts, not ad conventions. One shape each:

- **TikTok**: a creator to camera over a green-screen of the product or a review; a video reply to a real comment that voices the objection; POV ("POV: it's [moment] and [pain]"); "3 things I wish I knew before [buying X]"; a street or desk interview; a stitch-style reaction to a common claim.
- **Instagram Reels and Stories**: the TikTok forms, recut natively; no watermarks from other apps.
- **Meta feed static**: a native screenshot (notes app, text thread, a review); a founder note; us-vs-them (comparative claims substantiated and dated); a testimonial card (a real, attributed quote); feature callouts; a carousel whose first card is the hook.
- **YouTube**: the problem, the promise and the brand before the skip button appears; a tutorial or demo where intent is search-like; Shorts as TikTok.
- **LinkedIn**: a Thought Leader Ad, a member's own post run from their profile (a first-person insight, not a pitch); a document ad whose first page is the hook; a customer quote with name, title and company.
- **Display**: one line of about seven words, one image, the brand.

Each format is an execution, not a concept: tag it in the ad name.
