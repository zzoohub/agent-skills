# Post Skeletons

One skeleton per format. Use them after the Frame and Write steps in `references/social/guide.md`: each skeleton's asset slot is mandatory, every number comes from a source or carries `[SOURCE NEEDED: …]`, and the hook is written last. Budgets are ceilings; a strong asset needs fewer words. When a post argues for a change, PAS or BAB from `references/persuasion-frameworks.md` sets the order; most posts need only point → asset → reasoning. Launch-day versions for Show HN, Product Hunt and Reddit are in `references/social/platforms.md` § Launch-day copy.

## Text post (LinkedIn, Threads, Bluesky): ≤220 words

```
[Hook: the one concrete, unusual thing in this post]
[asset: the number, screenshot, quote or decision, stated plainly]
[How it happened or how we decided: 2-4 short paragraphs]
[What the reader can take from it, in their terms]
[Close on the goal: a question only the ICP can answer, a link, or nothing]
```

## X post or thread: ≤45 words a post, ≤8 posts

```
Post 1: [the claim + the asset attached], readable without expanding
Posts 2-N: [one step or one proof each]; stop when they stop adding
Last post: [the takeaway, or the link]
```

## Carousel or document (LinkedIn, Instagram): ≤10 slides, ≤25 words a slide

```
Slide 1: [the outcome or the question, in the ICP's words]
Slides 2-N: [one step, number or example per slide; the asset on its own slide]
Last slide: [what to do next; who made this]
```

## Short video (Reels, TikTok, Shorts): ≤60 seconds, about 150 spoken words

```
0-3 s:   [show the thing (screen, result) and say the topic in one line]
3-45 s:  [the asset: the demo, the steps, or the before and after; one idea]
Last 10 s: [the payoff, stated plainly; the next step]
On screen: [captions throughout; the topic line in the first frame]
```

## Long video (YouTube): outline ≤150 words

```
Title + thumbnail: one promise the video keeps (Test & Compare picks by watch time)
0-30 s:  [the result shown first; who this is for]
Chapters: [one step each, with the asset on screen]
End:     [what to do next; the one link in the description]
```

## Changelog post (company page): ≤120 words

```
[What changed, in the user's words, not the ticket's]
[Who it's for: the problem it removes]
[How to use it: the first step; a screenshot or clip]
[The link]
```

## Reddit or community post: ≤250 words

```
Title: [what you found or built, plainly; "I built" if you did]
[Who you are; the problem as this community describes it]
[What you tried or learned, with specifics and the asset]
[The product only where the rules allow, with its cost and limits]
[The question you want answered]
```

## Build-in-public update: ≤180 words

```
[The metric or decision and its period: "Month 7: churn rose to [SOURCE NEEDED: churn]"]
[What we expected vs what happened]
[What we changed, and why]
[What we'll report next time, even if it's worse]
```

## Reply: ≤80 words

```
[The answer or the counterexample, in the first line]
[One piece of evidence the post lacked: a number, case or experience]
[Optional: a question that moves the thread forward]
```
