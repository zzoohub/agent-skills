# Pillars, Calendar and Repurposing

Plan only what the poster can keep up for 12 weeks inside the hours they stated (`references/social/guide.md` § Decide). Keyword research, SEO topic clusters and blog calendars belong to the search-visibility capability, if available; this file plans social posts.

## Pillars

Two or three pillars, each tied to a moment that sends the ICP looking for help (a trigger) and to a steady source of assets:

| Pillar | ICP trigger | Asset source | A post it yields |
|---|---|---|---|
| The problem the product removes | "Month-end close starts" | Support tickets, sales calls | The fix for the commonest blocker |
| How we build and decide | "Comparing tools" | Design docs, postmortems | A decision and its reasons |
| Proof | "Justifying the purchase" | Customer results, with permission | One customer's before and after |

The rows are an example for a finance tool; derive yours from the ICP's triggers. A pillar with no asset source runs dry within a month: drop it.

## Post filter

Post an idea only if (1) it carries an asset only we have, (2) it addresses a pain the ICP describes in their own words, and (3) the poster has earned the right to say it: they did it, measured it or decided it. Two of three: park it. Fewer: drop it. Break: news in the niche, where speed outranks (1).

Ideas come from where the ICP talks: sales and support conversations (their phrases make the best hooks), the ICP's own communities, our data, and the decisions we made this week. Competitors' feeds show what is crowded, not what to say.

## Calendar

One calendar, four weeks, at most 12 rows. Plan the next four weeks only after reading the last four.

| Week | Day | Channel | Pillar | Asset | Angle | Goal metric | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Keep one slot a week open for what happened that week: a customer result, a release, a reply worth expanding. Schedule reply and community time too, in hours.

## Repurposing

Repurpose only what earned it: a post at 2× or more of the account median on its goal metric, or a long piece (talk, article, video) holding several distinct assets. Each derivative gets its own angle and native form (skeletons in `references/social/post-templates.md`), never a re-upload. Quote only the poster's own words or material used with permission.

| Source | Derivatives |
|---|---|
| Talk, webinar or long video | 3-5 clips, one idea each; a carousel of the steps; a text post on the strongest claim |
| Article or postmortem | A text post on the decision; a thread of the steps; a short video of the result |
| A post at 2× median or more | A carousel or video version for the other channel; a deeper follow-up a week later |

AI may draft derivatives; the poster checks every fact and keeps their voice.

## Launch calendar

| When | Action |
|---|---|
| −30 days | The maker takes part on the launch platforms as a person: comments, feedback to other makers, no launch asks |
| −14 days | Build-in-public posts on the problem and the decisions; a waitlist of people who asked to try it |
| −7 days | Launch-day copy drafted and reviewed (`references/social/platforms.md` § Launch-day copy); the try-it path tested without signup |
| −1 day | Thread owners and their hours confirmed; insiders briefed to disclose who they are if they comment, and never asked to vote |
| Launch days | One platform a day by default; announce the product on own channels; each thread staffed |
| +1-3 days | Answer what's left; fix what launch feedback found |
| +7 days | Post what happened and what changed, with numbers you'll keep reporting |
