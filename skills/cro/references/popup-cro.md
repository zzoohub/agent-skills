# Popup CRO

Popups, modals, slide-ins and banners for email capture, lead magnets, promotions and announcements. The email sequence after capture → copywriting, if available.

## Leak signatures

- **Captures up, site conversion down** → the popup interrupts buyers. Confirm: the holdout comparison on purchases or signups. Narrow the targeting (below).
- **Discount-driven captures, little downstream revenue** → discount hunters. Confirm: revenue per visitor vs the holdout, net of discount cost. Offer discounts to first-time visitors only, or switch to a non-discount incentive.
- **Mobile bounce up after launch** → a full-screen overlay on mobile. Confirm: bounce by device before vs after. Use a banner or slide-in that leaves the content visible.

## Judgment calls

- **Target away from buyers**: not on cart, checkout or pricing pages, for logged-in customers or for recent converters. Break only when a holdout shows the exception pays (a cart exit offer, net of discount cost).
- **Trigger on intent, not a timer.** A click on a lead-magnet link beats any timed popup; behavior triggers (a past pricing visit, scroll depth on long content) come next. Cursor-based exit intent works only on desktop; never hijack the mobile back button to fake it.
- **Frequency**: at most once per session; remember dismissals. Safari deletes script-set storage after 7 days without user interaction, so key long caps to a user ID.
- **Respect the dismiss**: a visible close, reachable by keyboard and Escape; a neutral decline ("No thanks"), never confirmshaming ("No, I don't want to save money").

## Compliance (verified 2026-10; not legal advice)

- **Marketing opt-in**: unticked by default, with a link to the privacy notice.
- **Storage consent**: in the EU (ePrivacy), triggers that store or read non-essential data (cart or pricing-visit detection, cross-page personalization, progressive profiling) need prior consent; gate them behind the consent banner, separately from the email opt-in. The UK (PECR Schedule A1, since 5 February 2026) exempts storage used only for usage statistics that improve the site, or to adapt or enhance how it looks and works, if users get clear information and a free way to object; confirm with counsel which triggers qualify.
- **Dismissal memory**: a session-length dismissal flag is generally exempt; persisting dismissals or caps for days without the user asking needs disclosure or consent in the EU (Article 29 Working Party, Opinion 04/2012, §3.6). Confirm with counsel.
- **Search**: Google's guidance says intrusive dialogs can hurt search performance on any device; never redirect to a separate page for consent or input.
